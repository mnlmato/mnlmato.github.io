Draw with your finger. There's a hologram underneath.

Playink turns your phone or tablet into magic scratch paper. Every stroke reveals a holographic foil that shines as you tilt your phone.

The app

Five backgrounds, and every drawing shines differently

How it works

Kids get it in seconds

  1. Draw

    Run your finger over the black layer.

  2. Reveal

    Every stroke uncovers the foil underneath.

  3. Tilt

    Move your phone and the light moves across the drawing.

  4. Switch it up

    The magic wand picks another foil.

For families

Made for a child to use on their own

Playink is part of the Google Play Families program. It collects no personal data and there is nothing to buy.

  • AdsNone
  • In-app purchasesNone
  • AccountNot needed
  • Personal dataNot collected
  • Crash reportsTechnical only, to fix bugs

The shiny backgrounds and video sharing need Android 13 or later. On earlier versions, every drawing reveals a color gradient.
