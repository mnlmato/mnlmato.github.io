Questions

Questions parents ask

Is Playink free?
Yes. Playink is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
What is scratch art?
Scratch art paper is a sheet covered with a black layer. When you scratch it, the colors underneath show through. Playink does the same on the screen, with a holographic foil underneath that moves as you tilt the phone.
Which devices does it work on?
Android phones and tablets with Android 7.0 or later. The shiny backgrounds and video sharing need Android 13 or later; on earlier versions every drawing reveals a color gradient.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Drawing, saving and sharing work without a connection.
Does my child need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no login.
Is there an iPhone version?
No. Playink is only available for Android, on Google Play.

Behind the hologram

How the holographic foil works

The foil isn't a video. It's a shader that works out the color of every pixel as you tilt the phone, imitating the colors of a soap bubble and the sparkle of glitter.
